Shipping Policy

Shipping guidance for a publication that does not directly dispatch products.

Opulent Carry Journal is an independent editorial publication. It does not currently sell or ship physical products directly.

No Direct Fulfilment

This section explains our approach to no direct fulfilment in clear, practical terms. Information is used only where necessary to operate the website, respond to messages, respect consent choices and meet applicable obligations. We do not invent transactional promises or transfer responsibility away from a retailer that completes a purchase.

If a linked third-party service is involved, its published terms and privacy practices govern that relationship. Readers should review those details before submitting personal information or completing a transaction. Questions may be sent to editorial@opulentcarryjournal.com or by post to our London address.
